Decadence Without Pleasure

Vocabulary Treatment

Do you want to have a good time? You know, really enjoy yourself? Click here, and tell us your desires. Maybe it's a new outfit, or some exotic1 cuisine2 delivered anonymously3 and steaming hot to your door. Maybe it's porn or gambling. Perhaps you prefer the infinite scroll, the endlessly unfurling4 ribbon of glossy images and videos starring people you'll never meet doing things you'll never do. Or maybe you favor talking to a chatbot who will assure you that you are the most special of all its users.

For anyone with an Internet connection and a little discretionary5 income, the contemporary6 moment offers a superabundance7 of seductive8 distractions. Why then the pervasive9 mood of emptiness? Why are so many at once overstimulated10 and bored?

Welcome to the era of decadence11 without pleasure.

Definitions

1. exotic: (eg-ZAH-tik) - originating in or characteristic of a distant foreign country; foreign, unfamiliar, exciting. The chef sourced exotic spices from three continents for the menu. ↑ back
2. cuisine: (kwih-ZEEN) - a style or method of cooking, especially as characteristic of a particular country or region; cooking, fare, culinary style. The restaurant specializes in authentic Thai cuisine. ↑ back
3. anonymously: (uh-NAH-nuh-mus-lee) - in a way that does not reveal one's name or identity; namelessly, secretly, incognito. The donor gave anonymously so no one would know who had funded the scholarship. ↑ back
4. unfurling: (un-FUR-ling) - unrolling or spreading out from a rolled or folded state; unrolling, unfolding, opening out. The flag kept unfurling in the wind as the ceremony began. ↑ back
5. discretionary: (dih-SKRESH-uh-nair-ee) - available to be spent or used as one chooses, rather than being required for necessities; optional, flexible, non-essential. After paying rent and bills, she had little discretionary income left. ↑ back
6. contemporary: (kun-TEM-puh-rair-ee) - belonging to or occurring in the present time; current, present-day, modern. Contemporary art often challenges traditional ideas of beauty. ↑ back
7. superabundance: (soo-per-uh-BUN-dunss) - an excessive or overflowing amount of something; excess, surplus, glut. The harvest brought a superabundance of vegetables that the farm could not sell in time. ↑ back
8. seductive: (sih-DUK-tiv) - tempting and attractive in a way that is hard to resist; tempting, alluring, enticing. The advertisement made a seductive promise of easy wealth. ↑ back
9. pervasive: (per-VAY-siv) - spreading widely throughout an area or group of people; widespread, prevalent, rampant. A pervasive sense of anxiety settled over the office after the layoffs were announced. ↑ back
10. overstimulated: (oh-ver-STIM-yuh-lay-tid) - excessively excited or aroused by too much stimulation; overwhelmed, overexcited, overloaded. By the end of the fair, the toddler was overstimulated and started crying. ↑ back
11. decadence: (DEK-uh-dunss) - luxurious self-indulgence, especially involving moral or cultural decline; excess, self-indulgence, dissipation. Critics described the lavish party as a symbol of the era's decadence. ↑ back
